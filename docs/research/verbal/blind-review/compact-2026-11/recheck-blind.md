# Blind review: rechecked items

For each item, choose the one statement the text supports. Label every other option False (the text contradicts it), Not supported (the text does not say it) or Overstated (stronger than the text). Use only the text.

## v15-03-c

In a fictional inquiry, an authentic customs stamp dates the stamped object's presence at a border, but a later reproduction of a stamp dates nothing about the copy's journey. A ledger entry quoting a crate number proves the crate's dispatch without identifying its contents. Seal numbers are never reused, and nothing can be put into a parcel without opening it.

The ledger records a crate dispatched on 4 June. An authenticated photograph at the border on 9 June shows a parcel bearing seal R inside that crate. A receipt dated 12 June records the first opening of that parcel since seal R was applied and lists a notebook inside. The notebook's cover carries a reproduced customs stamp dated 2 June; cover and stamp were made together on paper first available on 6 June, and the cover was attached before the notebook entered the parcel. All these sources are authentic, and all dates fall in one year. Nothing shows who packed the notebook or when the parcel was dispatched.

Which of the following statements is supported by the passage?

A. The notebook was packed between 6 and 9 June; the ledger dates only the crate's dispatch.
B. The reproduced customs stamp shows that the notebook itself crossed the border on 2 June.
C. The customs official seen in the 9 June photograph put the notebook into the parcel.
D. The cover was made on 6 June by the same workshop that supplied the parcel's seals.

## v15-05-c

A fictional journal lifts an embargo only when an eligible editor authorises release and the relevant data are available. Eligibility comes from a current appointment or a guest mandate. An appointment ends on resignation, even if the editor's email keeps working. A guest mandate covers only the subject it names and requires the publisher's written acceptance. Data are available through public deposition or controlled access. A repository counts as public only if readers can download the actual records without applying for permission; a public landing page is not enough. Controlled access requires an independent access committee and a binding obligation to decide applications within thirty days. A promise merely to consider applications sets no binding deadline.

The embargo on an ecology manuscript was validly lifted. Its authorising editor had resigned and had no new appointment, though the old email still worked. The records were held in a permission-only repository with a public landing page, and the provider itself had only promised to consider requests. No other route existed.

Which of the following statements is supported by the passage?

A. The public landing page made the records count as publicly deposited data.
B. The publisher probably chose the former editor because of earlier ecology work.
C. Release needed an accepted ecology mandate and an independent committee bound by a deadline.
D. The access committee received more applications after the embargo was lifted than before.

## v18-04-c

A fictional municipality issues debt through a standard route only when principal and interest are fixed. Linking either payment to a price index makes the instrument structured, even if a minimum payment is guaranteed. Structured issuance requires a treasury exemption or an independent suitability opinion. An exemption applies only to the issuing body it names and does not pass to a newly incorporated utility owned by that body. A suitability opinion requires a specialist authorised for structured instruments. It lapses if the specialist changes employer, until the new employer grants a fresh endorsement after reviewing prior work or after a new assessment. Both routes require an independence declaration concerning the proposed lender; one made before the lender was identified does not count.

A newly incorporated utility validly issued debt with indexed interest and a fixed minimum. The exemption named the municipality alone. The sole specialist, authorised for structured instruments, changed employer before giving the opinion, and the published declaration predated identification of the lender. No other route existed.

Which of the following statements is supported by the passage?

A. The fixed minimum kept the debt standard, so the utility could use the municipal exemption.
B. The endorsement could rely on the declaration made before the lender was known.
C. The new employer chose a fresh assessment over a review of the specialist's prior work.
D. A declaration naming the lender was made after the lender was identified.
