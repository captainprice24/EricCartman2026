## v19-01

A fictional institute releases a sensitive analysis only through a currently licensed laboratory or under an authorised external supervisor. Laboratory licensing ends when the laboratory changes legal owner. Reissue is possible, but requires its designated quality officer to pass a practical assessment after that ownership change. The quality officer cannot be one of the researchers whose analysis is being released. The public licensing list can lag behind valid reissues.

External supervisors qualify through a current advanced credential or a converted older credential. Advanced credentials issued before a method revision cease to be current. Conversion of an older credential requires a practical assessment after the revision. A supervisor with a newly issued advanced credential must also have passed a practical assessment after that revision. Supervisors, like quality officers, must be independent of the researchers. Neither a briefing attendance record nor an old credential establishes when such an assessment was passed.

The analysis was validly released after the laboratory changed owner. The method revision occurred before that ownership change. The public list still showed the former licence, and the only possible external supervisor held an older credential. The record does not establish whether the laboratory obtained reissue or used supervision. No other release or competence route existed.

Which of the following statements is supported by the passage?

A. The laboratory had received a reissued licence, so its designated quality officer rather than an external supervisor supplied the required current competence.
B. The older external credential had been converted, so the public list’s former licence entry correctly showed that the laboratory could not release independently.
C. An independent quality officer or external supervisor passed a practical assessment after the method revision; the permitted release route remains unspecified.
D. A briefing record or an assessment predating the method revision was sufficient for whichever competence route supported this particular sensitive release.

## v19-04

A corridor counts as separate only when it reaches an independent exit without rejoining the ordinary escape corridor. Rejoining it on a different floor does not qualify. A fire-pressure test requires a calibrated meter at the time of testing. Meter calibration ends after its sensing element changes. Renewal can be a workshop or field procedure, but both must compare the meter with a reference independently calibrated after that reference's latest repair. An old sticker does not survive a later repair without recertification.

The main building validly hosted trainees sleeping overnight. Only the annex held accreditation. The proposed additional corridor rejoined the ordinary corridor downstairs. The reference was repaired before the meter’s sensing element was replaced, and the pressure test followed both events. No other room, corridor, meter or clearance route existed.

## v19-05
