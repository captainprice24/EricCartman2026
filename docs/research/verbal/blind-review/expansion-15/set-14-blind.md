# Blind review: verbal-14

For each item, choose the one statement the text supports. Label every other option False (the text contradicts it), Not supported (the text does not say it) or Overstated (stronger than the text). Use only the text.

## v14-01

A fictional network issues a badge either for an ordinary placement or under a returning-trainee waiver. The waiver requires the previous placement to have ended within six weeks; otherwise a current agreement is necessary. An agreement takes effect only while its accommodation clearance remains valid. Clearance requires either a directly licensed sponsor or an approved property. A subsidiary's licence cannot be used by its parent.

Property approval normally requires an independent verifier's personal inspection. A remote review can replace that visit only if an independent verifier personally inspected the same building within the preceding year and the building has not since been converted. Conversion cancels earlier property approval and any clearance based on it. Every fresh approval after conversion must therefore satisfy the inspection rule again; an inspection by the trainee's mentor cannot count as independent. The register may omit supporting documents without invalidating a duly issued badge.

Noor's badge was validly issued nine weeks after her previous placement ended. Her sponsor was an unlicensed parent company with a licensed subsidiary. The property had been inspected eight months earlier, then converted before her new agreement took effect. The public register contains no approval or inspection record dated after the conversion. No other waiver or route exists.

Which of the following statements is supported by the passage?

A. Someone independent of Noor's mentor had inspected the accommodation in person after its conversion, despite the missing public inspection record.
B. Her sponsor could use its subsidiary's licence to obtain accommodation clearance without any fresh approval of the property after its conversion.
C. The person who authorised the new property approval had also carried out the inspection eight months before the conversion took place.
D. The property's earlier approval had expired before the conversion, so the alteration did not itself remove an approval still in force.

## v14-02

A fictional heritage fund pays a restoration grant only to a current custodian with an eligible project. A custodian may establish eligibility through a listed building or through a research partnership. A project on a listed building still needs conservation consent unless it is confined to removable furniture. The partnership route instead needs a university guarantee; a museum's promise is insufficient. Announcing an intended partnership does not create one.

Custodians who inherit an existing grant may retain the predecessor's eligibility decision, but only while the project and the building's use remain unchanged. Conversion from storage to public exhibition counts as a change of use even if nothing structural is altered. Changed use also cancels earlier conservation consent and any report supporting it; eligibility and any required consent must be established anew. Conservation consent can be granted after a site hearing or through written consultation; both require a report from an architect independent of the custodian. A contractor employed by the custodian cannot supply that report.

The fund validly paid Elin after she inherited a warehouse grant. Before payment she converted the building to public exhibition, and her project replaced its roof. The building remained listed. Her proposed university partnership had not been formed. The published payment summary contains no report or consent entry.

Which of the following statements is supported by the passage?

A. The proposed university partnership supplied a guarantee sufficient to avoid the conservation procedure for Elin’s replacement roof in the listed warehouse.
B. A fresh report by an independent architect was necessary before Elin’s grant could be validly paid for the converted warehouse.
C. The architect who assessed the project had also advised the previous custodian when the warehouse was used for storage.
D. All custodians inheriting a warehouse grant must obtain fresh conservation consent before continuing the restoration work approved under the original grant.

## v14-03

A fictional archive permits a restricted-file export when the recipient has a current research clearance and the export has either the depositor's permission or a public-interest authorisation. A clearance from a partner archive is accepted only if that archive has signed the current reciprocity schedule. Membership of the wider archive association is not a substitute. Permission may be delegated, but a change of legal ownership cancels earlier delegations.

The public-interest route requires two external assessors, except for exports of files already published in full. Publishing a catalogue description is not publication of a file. Both assessors must have access to the unredacted material; a confidentiality declaration alone cannot replace that access. The depositor may alternatively give fresh direct permission, but for a company in liquidation only its appointed liquidator can act. A former director cannot give new permission after liquidation begins.

A restricted export to Mara was validly completed after the depositing company entered liquidation. Mara's partner archive had not signed the reciprocity schedule, and the former director was the sole person offering depositor permission. No depositor permission had been given before liquidation. The files had appeared only as catalogue descriptions. No special exemption or further source of permission existed.

Which of the following statements is supported by the passage?

A. The former director preserved an earlier delegation by signing a fresh permission after the company entered liquidation, without intervention by its appointed liquidator.
B. The two external assessors reached the same view about the files’ historical value when they examined the unredacted material.
C. Mara’s clearance could not rest on the unsigned arrangement, and both external assessors needed access to the files without redactions.
D. Membership of the archive association alone supplied the unsigned partner archive with the recognition necessary for Mara’s existing clearance to be accepted.

## v14-04

A fictional basin authority renews an abstraction permit only when both flow compliance and meter assurance are current. Flow compliance follows either a recent gauge study or an ecological exemption. The exemption is available only to sites supplying households exclusively; a site supplying a commercial nursery is not exclusively domestic. Gauge studies remain usable for five years unless the intake is moved, when a fresh study is necessary regardless of distance.

Meter assurance requires either a certified sealed meter or a verified manual log. A broken seal cancels certification until an independent technician reseals the meter. Manual logs qualify only when checked against a second instrument calibrated after the most recent intake move. Both the technician and the calibrator must be independent of the permit holder. An employee's work can be technically accurate without meeting that independence condition. The authority's online summary records permits rather than every piece of supporting evidence.

A valid renewal was issued to a site serving homes and a nursery. Its intake had moved two months earlier, after its last published gauge study and instrument calibration. The meter seal remained broken at renewal, and nobody had resealed it. No exemption, emergency procedure or other assurance route applied.

Which of the following statements is supported by the passage?

A. The domestic customers allowed the site to use the ecological exemption even though the renewed permit continued to cover supply to the commercial nursery.
B. An employee of the permit holder was entitled to provide the independent calibration by certifying that the second instrument’s readings were technically accurate.
C. The manual log was probably more accurate than readings from a sealed certified meter would have been at the relocated intake.
D. Renewal required a post-relocation gauge study and a second instrument calibrated independently after the intake moved.

## v14-05

A fictional awards board hears an appeal only from an eligible applicant with standing. Eligibility may come from direct registration or a recognised consortium membership. Consortium recognition ends when its lead body withdraws; a former member cannot rely on the consortium's old certificate. Standing requires a timely objection unless the applicant was never served the provisional decision. A notice is served when delivered to the applicant's nominated address, even if staff do not open it.

A timely objection must arrive within ten days of service. It can be lodged by the applicant personally or by an authorised representative. A representative's authority must cover appeals explicitly; authority to submit the original bid is insufficient. A registered charity may use a simplified objection form, but this changes neither the deadline nor representation requirements. An appeal accepted under ordinary procedure has met these conditions, even if the public register omits the objection itself.

The board accepted Leto's appeal. The former consortium lead had already withdrawn. Notice had reached Leto's nominated address fourteen days before acceptance. Leto had lodged nothing personally; a consultant acted throughout. Leto's charitable status and original bid authority are recorded. No unserved-notice or other exception applied.

## v14-06

A fictional manufacturer closes a recall case only after every affected device has either been recovered or received a verified repair. A warehouse scan proves recovery only when it records the device's individual serial number; a crate identifier is insufficient. Repairs qualify through an accredited workshop or an independently witnessed mobile visit. Accreditation belongs to the named workshop, not to every member of its franchise.

A mobile witness must see both the component replacement and the final load test. A video can substitute for attendance only if it shows both stages continuously and bears a verified timestamp. A clip of the load test alone does not qualify. If a device was transferred to another owner, a repair record from before transfer remains valid only when the serial number was unchanged. A controller replacement that changes that number requires a fresh matching record. The database's public view can omit valid private repair records.

One device's recall case was validly closed after its controller and serial number changed. It remained at the customer's site, and no individual warehouse recovery scan existed. The repairer was an unaccredited franchise member. The only video showed the load test. No other recovery or repair route was available.

Which of the following statements is supported by the passage?

A. The repairer relied on another franchise workshop’s accreditation to validate the mobile visit without an independent witness observing the repair and final load test.
B. An independent witness had observed the qualifying component replacement and final load test, documented under the device’s new serial identification.
C. The witness who attended the component replacement also installed the controller that changed the device’s serial number.
D. Every device in the customer’s crate had been repaired before this individual recall case could be marked as complete.

## v14-08

External certificates lose recognition when the sample's seal is broken before receipt; a photograph of the sealed parcel at dispatch does not preserve recognition. Calibration remains valid for six months but is cancelled by a detector replacement. A fresh calibration can be performed in-house or externally; either route requires a reference sample certified after the detector's installation. The signatory must hold a current release authorisation. A deputy may sign during the usual signatory's absence, but only if the deputy's separate authorisation is current. Seniority or a general laboratory-access pass cannot substitute. Release records need not display calibration or staff-authorisation documents.

A batch was assayed and validly released after its detector was replaced. The external sample arrived with a broken seal, and no validated manual method existed for the substance. ## v14-09

A fictional procurement unit signs a contract after a standstill period unless a narrow urgency exception applies. Urgency waives the period only when an independent reviewer certifies that delay creates immediate physical danger. Financial loss alone cannot qualify. When the period is observed, signature requires proof that every unsuccessful bidder was notified; a notice posted on the unit's website does not count as individual delivery.

Individual notices can be delivered electronically or by registered post. Electronic delivery is effective only after the bidder's designated portal acknowledges receipt; an outgoing-message log is insufficient. Registered delivery requires a signed receipt from the bidder or its authorised mail agent. For a bidder in administration, the former directors cease to be authorised mail agents unless the administrator appoints them again. The standstill begins after the last effective individual delivery, not after the first dispatch.

A contract was validly signed under this procedure. Delay posed financial loss but no immediate physical danger, and no reviewer certified such danger. One unsuccessful bidder was in administration; its portal had issued no acknowledgment, and a former director signed its postal receipt. The public award summary contains no appointment by the administrator. No other delivery route or exception existed.

Which of the following statements is supported by the passage?

A. The former director had regained mail authority, and that notice’s effective delivery preceded the standstill’s beginning.
B. The website notice began the standstill before the last unsuccessful bidder received an individually effective notification at its designated portal or through registered post.
C. The administrator’s appointment of the former director was probably issued on the day the procurement unit first dispatched the registered notice.
D. The contract’s urgency certificate was signed by an independent reviewer who considered the financial losses an immediate physical danger.

## v14-10

A fictional software escrow agent releases source code only after a verified trigger and a valid beneficiary request. A trigger is either supplier insolvency or a service outage lasting more than seven consecutive days. Planned maintenance does not count towards outage duration, and two shorter outages cannot be joined across a day when service worked. A rumour of insolvency is not a verified trigger.

A beneficiary request requires a current subscription or a surviving perpetual-access clause. The clause survives termination only if the beneficiary had deposited the final contract version before termination. Depositing an earlier draft does not qualify. Subscription renewal is effective only once payment clears; an invoice or pending transfer is not payment. Requests can be signed by a director or a delegated officer. Delegation expires when the requesting company changes legal form, unless the new board ratifies it after that change.

The agent validly released code to a company that had changed legal form. Its subscription had expired before the renewal payment, which remained pending, and officer Veda signed using a delegation originally given before the change. Its only outage lasted six days excluding maintenance. No other trigger, request route or exception applied. The public release record lists no contract deposit or ratification.

Which of the following statements is supported by the passage?

A. The pending subscription payment supported release because the six-day outage qualified for the outage trigger once the duration of planned maintenance was included.
B. Verified insolvency, timely deposit of the final contract, and renewed signing authority were necessary for the release to Veda’s company.
C. The new board ratified Veda’s delegation before it received confirmation of the supplier’s insolvency from the escrow agent.
