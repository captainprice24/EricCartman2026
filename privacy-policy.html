Privacy policy · EU Reasoning Trainer

Effective date: 3 November 2026

EU Reasoning Trainer is published by Prometheus Technologies.

Information on your device

The extension stores practice answers, progress, preferences, history and unfinished sessions in local storage in your Chrome profile. This information supports the trainer’s features. It is not transmitted to the publisher or synchronised between devices. Website and extension progress are separate.

Collection and sharing

The extension does not collect or transmit personal information and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking services. It does not sell user data. It does not access your browsing history or the contents of other websites.

Retention and deletion

Local information remains until you reset the relevant trainer data, clear the extension’s stored data or uninstall the extension. Clearing a browser profile also removes its saved information. Normal extension updates are intended to preserve progress; removing and reinstalling the extension can erase it.

External links and browser services

All app code and questions are included in the extension. The trainer works offline and initiates no remote requests during practice. If you choose to open an external reference or website link, that website’s privacy policy applies. Chrome’s extension installation, store and update services are operated by Google under Google’s policies.

Support correspondence

If you email the publisher, the details you voluntarily supply are used to respond to your request. Support correspondence is separate from the extension and is handled through the publisher’s email provider. Avoid sending sensitive information. You may request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any applicable retention obligations.
