# Blind review: verbal-12

For each item, choose the one statement the text supports. Label every other option False (the text contradicts it), Not supported (the text does not say it) or Overstated (stronger than the text). Use only the text.

## v12-01-c

A fictional translation office marks a text as linguistically approved when both a translator and an independent reviser sign the final version. A subject expert's comments can inform either signatory but cannot replace either signature. Any change made after revision must be checked again by the reviser before the changed version is approved, however minor the correction. Approval is separate from authorisation to publish, which belongs to the commissioning body. That body may publish an unapproved version on its own responsibility, but this does not retrospectively supply the missing linguistic approval.

The translator and reviser signed version A on Monday. On Tuesday the translator changed a number, producing version B. On Wednesday the reviser checked and signed B, but the translator expressly withdrew their own signature pending another source check. The commissioning body then authorised publication of B. No further translator signature or waiver exists. Version A remains unchanged in the archive, and neither signatory has withdrawn a signature from it.

Which of the following statements is correct?

A. A's signatures carry over to B once the reviser has checked the changed number.
B. A keeps both signatures; B is cleared to publish yet lacks linguistic approval.
C. The translator withdrew on finding that the new number differed from the expert's source.
D. The reviser's signature on B stands in for the signature the translator withdrew.

## v12-02-c

A fictional manuscript project distinguishes the wording of a text from the dates of the physical copies carrying it. Copies K and L both contain a corrected phrase that is absent from M. The catalogue notes that any copy carrying this correction must descend, directly or indirectly, from an amended exemplar. It does not say that every descendant reproduces every amendment, since scribes sometimes omitted passages. The team has ruled out independent invention of the phrase in K and L, although the exemplar itself has not been found.

An ownership note appears in K and M but not in L. Because ownership notes could be added independently after copying, the shared note does not establish a common textual ancestor more recent than the amended exemplar. Physical testing dates K later than M but supplies no relative date for L, and the dating concerns the surviving copies, not their source texts. The catalogue gives no complete copying tree.

Which of the following statements is correct?

A. K and M share a more recent textual source than K and L, as only they carry the note.
B. L was produced after M, since it shares its correction with K, the later of those two.
C. K and L descend from an amended exemplar, but the full copying order remains open.
D. M cannot descend from the amended exemplar, because each descendant keeps the correction.

## v12-03-c

A fictional report describes two laboratories that independently repeated a plant-growth experiment, using different plants in different buildings. Both found taller plants under treatment than under control. Each randomised treatment within its own trial, and its analyst worked from a coded file without knowing which code meant treatment until the calculations were complete.

Both laboratories, however, calibrated their height sensors with the same reference rod, supplied by the treatment manufacturer and later found to be longer than stated. The report does not give the resulting error or say whether it affected treatment and control readings differently. It also does not establish that the sensor calibration produced the observed treatment differences. A manual check found a treatment-control difference in one laboratory, but it covered only a subset of plants selected after the sensor results were known, and no selection rule was released. The manufacturer nevertheless called the findings three fully independent confirmations.

Which of the following statements is correct?

A. Both trials share a possible sensor bias; the manual check is no third independent trial.
B. The rod error inflated treatment readings more than control readings in both laboratories.
C. Coded analysis removed the shared calibration problem, since analysts could not spot treatment.
D. The manufacturer chose the manual subset to hide a negative result in the other laboratory.

## v12-04-c

A fictional district compared a supplier's estimate of the fuel saved by a new scheduling system with an independent consultant's report. The supplier's estimate used a projected reduction in vehicle idling. The consultant independently checked the fuel-per-minute coefficient, but took the projected reduction in idling directly from the supplier; its contract covered coefficient validation, not identification of the causal change in idling.

A fleet log recorded fewer total idling minutes after deployment, but over the same period the fleet operated fewer vehicles and completed fewer journeys. No per-journey or per-vehicle comparison was published, and no comparison group kept the old schedule. Both estimates concerned the change attributable to scheduling, not simply the observed fall in total minutes. The district nevertheless described the coefficient check and the fleet log as two independent confirmations of the system's full fuel-saving effect. No report claimed that the supplier's projection was wrong; the question was which parts had received independent support.

Which of the following statements is correct?

A. Validating the coefficient independently confirmed the complete fuel-saving estimate.
B. The fleet log proves idling per journey fell, as both idling minutes and journeys fell.
C. The coefficient was checked independently, but no source isolates the scheduling effect.
D. The consultant reused the whole calculation and checked none of its components itself.

## v12-05-c

## v12-06-c

## v12-07-c

D. The employers' expansion explains the current employment status of every nonrespondent.

## v12-08-c

A fictional clinic randomly assigned consenting patients to a text-reminder policy or an email-reminder policy. The study did not recruit patients who declined electronic reminders. The text group had fewer missed appointments. Each patient remained in the assigned group for analysis whether or not the message was delivered or read, nobody switched groups, and outcomes were recorded for every enrolled patient.

Some emails failed, and some texts reached phones whose owners did not open them; reading could not be measured consistently for either medium. The policies also differed in whether the message contained a link to an appointment map: the text policy included that link, while the email policy did not. The clinic did not randomise medium and map link separately. The report compared the assigned groups, not people known to have read a reminder. A follow-up that would randomise map links independently and recruit more widely has been proposed, but has produced no findings.

Which of the following statements is correct?

A. With every outcome recorded, the trial isolates the effect of reading a text, not an email.
B. Excluding failed deliveries from each group would isolate the effect of the map link.
C. The text policy would also cut missed appointments among patients who refuse reminders.
D. The result compares two bundled policies and isolates neither reading nor the map link.

## v12-10-c

Packages awaiting release cannot be used even when already booked; staff must substitute released stock or reschedule. Package-level checking established that exactly 100 packages were in both the pending-release and committed groups. All packages outside the pending-release group had passed quality release. Of the packages in neither excluded group, 150 were confirmed unexpired, while expiry for the rest of that remainder was unresolved. No package was distributed or newly committed before the reserve snapshot, and the controller has not inferred expiry from the absence of a flag.

Which of the following statements is correct?

A. Usable reserve lies between 150 and 400 packages, depending on unresolved expiry checks.
B. The reserve holds exactly 300 packages once both excluded totals are subtracted from 800.
C. All 400 packages outside the two excluded groups count as usable reserve stock.

## v13-02-c

A fictional transport panel asked the same 100 registered members a binary support-or-oppose question about a tram proposal in March and September, with unchanged wording. One commentary claimed that the second survey merely contained a different mix of the original supporters, with nobody changing sides; another attributed any switches to the campaign. ## v13-03-c

## v13-04-c

## v13-05-c
