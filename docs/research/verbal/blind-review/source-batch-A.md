# Blind review: verbal-10

For each item, choose the one statement the text supports. Label every other option False (the text contradicts it), Not supported (the text does not say it) or Overstated (stronger than the text). Use only the text.

## v10-01

Passenger ferries follow a separate procedure. Fuel carriers count as freight vessels, and local waivers are never issued for fuel carriage. The harbour's public arrivals log records successful admissions but does not list deposits, inspections or priority certificates. On one recorded night it listed 18 arrivals, six of them freight vessels. A vessel can appear in that log more than once during a month.

The Silver arrived carrying fuel during that night's freight window. The harbour confirms that it admitted the vessel in accordance with the stated entry rules, with no emergency deviation. Its publicly available file contains no priority certificate and no technician's report. The file is a selection of documents released for public inspection rather than a complete record of documents held by the harbour. No rule makes public release of an inspection report a condition of entry.

Which of the following statements is correct?

A. Silver could lawfully have entered without inspection if its priority certificate was held in the harbour's unreleased records.
B. Every freight arrival in the night log required an active sensor, since waivers were unavailable for the Silver's particular cargo.
C. Silver's admission entails a passed inspection, but the night log does not establish that every other freight arrival was inspected.
D. The inspected freight vessels admitted that night were all fuel carriers, since that cargo category could not receive a local waiver.
