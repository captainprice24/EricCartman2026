# Blind review: rechecked items (second round)

For each item, choose the one statement the text supports. Label every other option False (the text contradicts it), Not supported (the text does not say it) or Overstated (stronger than the text). Use only the text.

## v10-09-c

A fictional heritage charter normally prohibits attaching signs to a protected facade. A temporary event permit can authorise a removable sign if the heritage officer approves the attachment method; the event permit alone does not approve the sign. Signs fixed into original stone are outside this exception, even if they will later be removed. The charter does not restrict free-standing signs in the courtyard, provided that the public route remains unobstructed.
 For one event, the officer approved unpierced clamps for a facade banner. Before installation, the venue switched to bolts fixed into original stone, keeping the same event permit. Its courtyard sign is free-standing, but chairs have been placed across the public route beside it. The venue now proposes removing the chairs while keeping the bolts. No new method approval or exception covers the bolts, and nothing else would obstruct the route once the chairs are gone. These provisions are the complete relevant rules.

Which of the following statements is correct?

A. The clamp approval also covers the bolts, since both fix the same banner for the same event.
B. Clearing the chairs cures the courtyard problem, but the bolts stay outside the exception.
C. Once the venue holds an event permit, all of its sign placements become permissible.
D. The venue changed its fixing after trial clamps had slipped in strong wind.

## v11-10-c

A fictional statistics unit lets analysts use de-identified survey files after confidentiality training, but linking them to employment records also requires approval from a linkage panel. An approval names both a project and a version of its linkage plan. Even replacing a variable with a less detailed one does not remove the need to seek approval for an amended plan; the panel decides whether the change can be handled through a simplified review, which is still a review rather than an exemption.
 The panel has approved a team's new seasonal-work project for annual employment status under a revised plan. The decision explicitly excludes monthly status. The team's older job-change project also holds a valid approval for annual data, but no decision transfers approval between projects. The team now proposes running the seasonal project on monthly observations, publishing only annual totals and keeping its existing secure environment. No approval covers those monthly inputs, and the panel has not said that annual outputs lift the input restriction.

Which of the following statements is correct?

A. No approval covers the monthly inputs, whatever the secure environment or annual outputs.
B. The older approval extends to the monthly plan because the secure environment is unchanged.
C. The team chose monthly observations because annual data hid seasonal changes in employment.
D. Publishing annual totals makes the use of monthly inputs authorised under the decision.

## v15-09-c

A fictional expedition authenticates a specimen's depth only if its capture cartridge stayed sealed from sampling until laboratory receipt. A cartridge may be opened during transfer only if a trained observer watches continuously and applies a newly logged seal before handling stops. A camera can replace the observer only if it records the cartridge interior; footage of a closed box's exterior is insufficient. At receipt, the laboratory must match the current individual seal, not merely the box number. A species can be identified from a fragment without establishing depth. An observer's training authorisation ends when the sampling protocol changes, unless a supplementary assessment under the new protocol is passed; field experience is no substitute. The public catalogue omits most seal and personnel records.

A specimen's depth was validly authenticated after its cartridge was opened at transfer. The only recording showed the outside of its transport box. Observer Tavi watched continuously, but Tavi's published training certificate predates a protocol change made before this transfer. No other observer or exception applied.

Which of the following statements is supported by the passage?

A. The exterior recording was an acceptable substitute for an authorised observer.
B. Tavi's field experience kept the training authorisation valid after the protocol change.
C. Tavi had passed the supplementary assessment, and the new seal was matched at receipt.
D. The public catalogue leaves out all of the expedition's seal and personnel records.
